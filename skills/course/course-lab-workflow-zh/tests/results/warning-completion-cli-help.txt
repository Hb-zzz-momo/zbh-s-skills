usage: audit_run_outputs.py [-h] [--notebook NOTEBOOK] [--log LOG]
                            [--stderr-log STDERR_LOG]
                            [--expected-code-cells EXPECTED_CODE_CELLS]
                            [--reviews REVIEWS] [--checklist CHECKLIST]
                            [--diagnostics-only]

Read-only runtime diagnostic and completion-evidence gate (stdlib only). Scans
full files, never executes or edits experiment code. This checks common
diagnostics and evidence consistency, not correctness or requirement coverage.

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --notebook NOTEBOOK
  --log LOG
  --stderr-log STDERR_LOG
  --expected-code-cells EXPECTED_CODE_CELLS
  --reviews REVIEWS
  --checklist CHECKLIST
  --diagnostics-only
